About Me

About Me

I grew up with Tamil film music playing constantly at home, the kind of music that tells you exactly what a scene is feeling before a single word is spoken. That's probably where my fascination with music and story started, before I had language for it.

I'm a Music Business student at Berklee with a Film Scoring minor, and most of my work lives at the intersection of those two things: understanding how music gets placed in film and TV, and understanding why certain music works in a scene in the first place. Sync licensing pulls me in because it's one of the few corners of the industry where the creative and business sides actually have to talk to each other. A supervisor has to feel something about a song and also clear it, budget it, license it. I want to be in that overlap.

Right now I'm building toward a career in sync, and eventually music supervision, learning the mechanics — metadata, royalties, PRO structures — alongside the instinct for what a scene actually needs. I want to be someone who can hear a cue and also handle the paperwork behind it.
